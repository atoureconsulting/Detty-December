# Sponsor targets — West African live events

A standing list, not tied to one event. Côte d'Ivoire first, then Ghana and Bénin.

Every name in Tier 1 and Tier 2 has been seen on the sponsor band of a real,
ticketed event. Tier 3 is research-led and unproven. The point of the split is
that a company already paying for a concert in Abidjan is a far shorter
conversation than a category leader who has never done it.

## The rule this list is organised around

**Never take a purely in-kind deal.** Rooms do not pay the insurance premium,
the visa charges or the production invoices, and they do not pay the management
fee. Every entry is marked for what it can realistically give.

- **CASH** — has a marketing budget and can sign a sponsorship fee.
- **IN KIND** — gives goods or services. Budget relief, not budget.
- **ACCESS** — gives permits, venues, press and introductions. Slowest to move,
  cheapest to ask.

Target shape: at least one CASH partner per category, with IN KIND layered
underneath to reduce what the cash has to cover.

## How to update

Every West African event poster carries its full sponsor band at the foot. When
one comes past:

1. Add the event to **Sources** with a new code.
2. Add any new company to **Tier 2** with that code.
3. If a company in Tier 2 now has two codes, move it to **Tier 1**.
4. If a Tier 3 name appears on a poster, move it up and note what it sponsored.

Tier 1 is the list that matters. It is the one built from behaviour rather than
from reputation.
